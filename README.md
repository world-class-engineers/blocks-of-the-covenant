# Blocks of the Covenant

![Blocks of the Covenant](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/world-class-engineers/blocks-of-the-covenant/main/resource_packs/blocks-of-the-covenant/pack_icon.png)

**Build the stories of the Bible. Walk through them block by block.**

Blocks of the Covenant is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on that brings the world of
Scripture into your hands. Place the sacred furnishings of the Tabernacle in
your builds, stand before the burning bush, watch the Nile turn to blood, and
follow the pillar of fire through the wilderness — then keep going, as the
library grows to span the whole biblical story.

The journey begins with the **Exodus and the forty years in the wilderness**:
the plagues of Egypt, the Red Sea, the Tabernacle and its holy vessels. From
there, the add-on will grow outward through the rest of the Bible — the
patriarchs, the judges, the kings, the prophets, and beyond — until every story
can be built, explored, and retold in Minecraft.

## Available Now

### The Temple Menorah

_“And thou shalt make a candlestick of pure gold… six branches shall come out
of the sides of it.”_ — Exodus 25:31–32

The seven-branched lampstand of hammered gold, fashioned after the pattern
shown to Moses on the mountain — the light that burned continually in the
Tabernacle and later in the Temple of Jerusalem. Crafted as a two-block-tall
decorative piece, ready to sanctify your Holy Place, synagogue, or study
build.

## Coming Soon

### The Exodus

- **The Burning Bush** — the bush that burned and was not consumed
- **The Ten Plagues of Egypt** — blood, frogs, gnats, flies, pestilence,
  boils, hail, locusts, darkness, and the slaying of the firstborn
- **The Parting of the Red Sea** — walls of water to the right and to the left
- **The Pillar of Cloud by Day and the Pillar of Fire by Night** — the
  presence that led Israel through the wilderness

### The Tabernacle and Its Furnishings

- **The Ark of the Covenant** — the mercy seat between the cherubim
- **The Table of Showbread** — the bread of the Presence
- **The Altar of Incense** — golden, before the veil
- **The Bronze Altar and the Bronze Laver** — for the court of the Tabernacle
- **And more** — curtains of blue, purple, and scarlet; the boards, the veil,
  and the court

### And Beyond

The wilderness is only the beginning. Future updates will reach across the
whole sweep of Scripture, until the stories of the Bible can be built and
experienced from beginning to end.
